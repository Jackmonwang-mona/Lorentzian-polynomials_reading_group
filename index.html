01 · About

A shared place for the work

This site keeps the project’s purpose, meetings, notes, and responsibilities visible in one place.

Our goal is to explore the properties and applications of Lorentzian polynomials. An important aspect of this project is to understand the bounded ratios for Lorentzian polynomials and their implications in various mathematical contexts.

Lorentzian polynomials Bounded ratios Convex geometry Algebraic combinatorics
