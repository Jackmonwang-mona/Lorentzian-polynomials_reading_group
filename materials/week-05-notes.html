From \(c\)-Rayleigh Polynomials to \(M\)-Convex Support

The two-part framework for proving that \(L_n^d\) is closed.

General proof framework

We introduced the concept of \(c\)-Rayleigh polynomials and divided the proof that \(L_n^d\) is closed into two main parts:

  1. Prove that every polynomial in \(L_n^d\) is \(c\)-Rayleigh (Proposition 2.19).
  2. Prove that a homogeneous \(c\)-Rayleigh polynomial has \(M\)-convex support (Theorem 2.23).
\[ f\in L_n^d \Longrightarrow f\text{ is }c\text{-Rayleigh} \Longrightarrow \operatorname{supp}(f)\text{ is }M\text{-convex}. \]

What we proved last week

Last week, we completed the first part of the framework. The argument can be summarized as follows.

  • In the degree-two case, if the Hessian has exactly one positive eigenvalue, then the polynomial is \(1\)-Rayleigh.
  • The same idea extends to arbitrary degree and gives the \(2(1-1/d)\)-Rayleigh property.
  • Therefore, it remains to prove that the Hessian of every polynomial in \(L_n^d\) has exactly one positive eigenvalue (Theorem 2.16(2)).

The homotopy in Theorem 2.13 shows that \(\mathring{L}_n^d\) is connected. Consequently, the number of positive eigenvalues cannot change along the homotopy once we know the following two facts:

  • The Hessian is nonsingular everywhere in \(\mathring{L}_n^d\) (Theorem 2.16(1)), so no eigenvalue can pass through zero.
  • There is at least one polynomial in \(\mathring{L}_n^d\) whose Hessian has exactly one positive eigenvalue. Proposition 2.14 supplies such examples: nonzero stable polynomials with nonnegative coefficients have this Hessian signature.

Today's goal

We now turn to the second part of the framework. To give a slightly different perspective from the paper, we will introduce a modified notion called \(M^{\#}\)-convexity. It is motivated by the independent sets of matroids and provides additional intuition for the proof.

We will then use an elegant minimal-counterexample argument to prove that the support is \(M\)-convex, completing the second part of the framework.
